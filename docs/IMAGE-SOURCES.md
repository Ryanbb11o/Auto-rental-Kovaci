# Image provenance

All three raster assets were created with the built-in Imagegen tool for this project on 3 November 2026. They are illustrative assets, not photographs of the company's actual fleet or verified exact locations. Source PNGs remain in the local generated-images library. Project copies are optimized to WebP without changing their content.

## Final project assets

- `dist/assets/hero.webp` — cinematic graphite car on an Albanian-inspired coastal mountain road.
- `dist/assets/fleet.webp` — three-panel studio composition used as a CSS sprite for city, comfort and SUV categories.
- `dist/assets/coastal-road.webp` — Albanian-Riviera-inspired travel editorial image.

The logo is a code-native typographic design, not an Imagegen output. Manrope is distributed under the SIL Open Font License; the complete licence is included in `dist/assets/manrope-license.txt`.

## Final prompts

### Hero

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: ultra-wide cinematic hero photograph for a premium local Albanian car-rental website. Create a photorealistic high-end automotive editorial image, landscape 16:9 composition. A beautiful metallic dark graphite grey modern sporty executive sedan, no visible logos, parked on a wide empty coastal mountain road in Albania, facing toward the left, front three-quarter view. Car occupies lower-right and middle-right of frame, whole car visible with detailed realistic wheels. Dramatic Albanian limestone mountains and a glimpse of distant blue-grey Adriatic water in the background, quiet dry grass and road barriers. Early evening blue hour with a tiny warm horizon and subtle red taillight accent, cinematic subdued grey tones, premium quiet travel mood. Leave generous uncluttered dark negative space across upper half and left third for white website typography. Low camera angle, 50mm automotive photography, natural physical reflections, sharp car, atmospheric landscape. NO text, no typography, no watermarks, no people, no private jet. This is a background image asset, not a screenshot or a designed website.

### Vehicle styles

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: car rental website vehicle-category contact sheet. Create one wide landscape photo with THREE EQUAL VERTICAL PANELS side by side in a seamless pale light grey minimalist automotive studio. Each third is a complete isolated realistic car in front three-quarter view facing left, centered, generous space around it, tiny soft shadow on studio floor. LEFT THIRD: metallic silver compact European five-door hatchback, understated clean styling. CENTER THIRD: graphite dark grey premium executive sedan. RIGHT THIRD: pearl grey compact modern family SUV. All cars fully visible inside their individual thirds, consistent lighting, same perspective and scale. Editorial catalogue photography, crisp professional real materials, silver/graphite/black color palette. Image aspect ratio 3:1, horizontal, each third has an identical light grey background. No dividing borders, NO text, NO badges, NO logos, NO watermark. This is a source image for three CSS-cropped car category photos, not a website design.

### Coast

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: travel editorial website photograph. A vertical 4:5 cinematic aerial travel photograph inspired by the Albanian Riviera: an elegant serpentine paved coastal road curves through rugged limestone green hills above a calm Adriatic turquoise bay, distant layered mountains. Small dark grey car on the road for scale. Subtle natural late afternoon sunlight, editorial travel magazine quality, real natural texture, refined slightly muted colors. The composition should lead the eye along the road from lower left to the sea upper right. No buildings close to camera, no people close to camera, no text, no logo, no watermark. This is illustrative travel imagery for a car rental company in Albania, not a precise named location.
